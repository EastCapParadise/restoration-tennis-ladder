End of Season Tournament

Men's Open · Women's Open · Men's Club — finals night Friday, Nov 6 at Stratford Park, Arlington

Three draws close out the season: a 16-player Men's Open, a 16-player Women's Open, and the Men's Club, a four-player round robin for our highest-rated men. Seeds 1–16 in both Open draws are set entirely by ladder points, with strength of schedule breaking ties — the same tiebreak the rankings page uses. Rating has no effect on Open seeding or bracket placement. The Club 4 are picked the other way round: by rating alone.

Every round before finals night is self-scheduled. Find your opponent in the directory, agree on a court and a time, play, and report it through Report Match — tournament matches count for the ladder like any other match.

Format

Draws
16 men and 16 women, single elimination, seeded 1–16. Plus the Men's Club: the four highest-rated men by display rating, round robin.
Seeding
Ladder points, highest first. Ties break on strength of schedule (SOS), highest first — the same rule as the main rankings. Seeds are frozen at the Oct 11 standings snapshot and do not move after that, even though the ladder keeps running through Nov 1.
Quarterfinal groups
Each quarter of the draw is a self-scheduling group of four — seeds 1, 8, 9, 16 / 4, 5, 12, 13 / 3, 6, 11, 14 / 2, 7, 10, 15. Three matches per group (two Round of 16, then the quarterfinal between their winners) produce one semifinalist. Sort your three matches out among yourselves; there is no assigned order beyond playing the Round of 16 before the quarterfinal.
Match length
One set is the default for every match through the semifinals. Players may mutually agree to play more — a pro set or a full match — if both want it. Finals night is 8-game pro sets, no exceptions.
Scheduling
The Round of 16, quarterfinals, and semifinals are all self-scheduled by the players, at a court and time you agree on. Only finals night is fixed.
Defaults and walkovers
Any match not played by its deadline goes to the higher seed. This applies inside the quarterfinal groups too. If a walkover decision ever runs into a seed tie, SOS breaks it — same rule as seeding.
Dropouts — Women's Open
The women's draw has exactly 16 ladder participants and no alternates. A dropout is a walkover: her opponent advances and nothing is re-drawn.
Dropouts — Men's Open
The men's draw backfills dropouts with the next seeds — 17, 18, and so on, in the same ladder-points-then-SOS order. Backfill happens on Oct 13, once opt-outs are in.
Men's Club round robin
Four players, everyone plays everyone once: six matches, three per player, hard deadline Nov 4. Standings break ties in this order — wins, then head-to-head, then fewest games lost. The top two advance to the club final.

Finals Night

Friday, Nov 6 — Stratford Park, Arlington. Two lighted courts and bleachers, so everything runs on site with somewhere to sit and watch.

  • 8:00 PM — Women's Open final and Men's Open final, played simultaneously on both courts. 8-game pro sets.
  • 9:15 PM — Men's Club final as the nightcap, one court, everyone watching.

Semifinalists must confirm their Nov 6 availability by Nov 4. If a finalist can't make it, the semifinal loser steps in. Rain date is Saturday, Nov 7, same times and same venue.
