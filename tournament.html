End of Season Tournament

Men's Open · Women's Open · Men's Club — finals night Friday, Nov 6 at Stratford Park, Arlington

Three draws close out the season. The Men's Open and Women's Open are 16-player single-elimination draws, seeded 1–16 by ladder points. The Men's Club is a four-player round robin for our four highest-rated men, with the top two meeting in a club final.

Open seeds come from ladder points alone, with strength of schedule breaking ties — the same rule as the rankings page — and are frozen at the Oct 11 standings snapshot. Everything before finals night is self-scheduled, and tournament matches count for the ladder like any other match.

Format

Match length
One set is the default for every match through the semifinals. Players may mutually agree to play more — a pro set or a full match — if both want it. Finals night is 8-game pro sets, no exceptions.
Self-scheduling
The Round of 16, quarterfinals, and semifinals are all self-scheduled. Find your opponent in the directory, agree on a court and a time, play, and report it through Report Match. Only finals night is fixed.
Quarterfinal pods
Each quarter of the Open draws is a pod of four players — seeds 1, 8, 9, 16 / 4, 5, 12, 13 / 3, 6, 11, 14 / 2, 7, 10, 15. The pod is only a scheduling convenience so the four of you can coordinate court time together. It's still straight single elimination: two Round of 16 matches, then a quarterfinal between the two winners.
Deadlines
Any match not played by its deadline goes to the higher seed.
Coordinating with Michael
In addition to you scheduling among yourselves — not instead of it — Michael, the tournament director, will text each quarterfinal pod's group chat (or each of you individually) to help get matches on the calendar. Text him the result of every Round of 16 and quarterfinal match as soon as it's played, as well as entering it in Report Match, so he isn't left to find out later.

Finals Night

Friday, Nov 6 — Stratford Park, Arlington. Two lighted courts and bleachers, so everything runs on site with somewhere to sit and watch.

  • 8:00 PM — Women's Open final and Men's Open final, played simultaneously on both courts. 8-game pro sets.
  • 9:15 PM — Men's Club final as the nightcap, one court, everyone watching.

Semifinalists must confirm their Nov 6 availability by Nov 4. Rain date is Saturday, Nov 7, same times and same venue.
